The Mental Model: Punctuation in business names performs two entirely different functions: it either contracts a single word (like an apostrophe), or it bridges two separate words (like a hyphen). Therefore, treating all punctuation with the same "replace with space" rule will always break one of these structures.

The Universal Normalization Funnel
To make text cleaning bulletproof, your normalization module must run incoming strings through a strict, five-stage sequential funnel:

1. Semantic Expansion
Before touching any punctuation, convert universally understood symbols into their word equivalents.

Action: Translate the ampersand (&) into the word AND.

Without this: A & B becomes A B, which loses the semantic glue and might fail token subset checks against A AND B.

2. The Erasure Tier (Contractions)
Target the specific punctuation marks used to shorten or combine single words.

Action: Completely delete apostrophes, single quotes, and backticks. Do not replace them with spaces.

With this feature: INT'L fuses seamlessly into INTL, ensuring it gets caught by your abbreviation dictionary. NIG'A becomes NIGA.

3. The Substitution Tier (Bridges)
Handle every other non-alphanumeric character in existence.

Action: Apply a universal filter that flags anything that is not a letter from A-Z or a number from 0-9, and converts it into a single space.

With this feature: You never have to manually hardcode brackets, slashes, asterisks, or hyphens again. HORLA-MAT splits safely into HORLA MAT. PRODECO (PROPERTY) becomes PRODECO PROPERTY.
